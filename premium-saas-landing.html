Features

Built for teams that
ship faster than they meet.

Every feature in Lumen is designed to remove a step between asking a question and acting on the answer.

✦

Ask in plain English

Type "Which feature drove last week's signups?" and Lumen returns a chart, the contributing segments, and a one-line summary you can paste into Slack.

◈

Live dashboards

Build dashboards in minutes with drag-and-drop tiles. Numbers refresh in real time as events arrive — no more stale screenshots in Monday standup.

▲

Funnels & cohorts

Trace users from first touch to conversion. Build cohorts by behavior, attribute, or campaign, and watch them evolve over weeks.

◉

Smart alerts

Lumen learns what normal looks like for your metrics and pings you in Slack the moment something moves outside the expected range.

◆

Collaborative reports

Share a report with a link. Comment on any chart. Mention teammates. Decisions stay attached to the data they came from.

◐

Warehouse-native

Connect Lumen to Snowflake, BigQuery, or Redshift. Your data stays in your warehouse — Lumen queries it directly, no ETL required.

How it works

From signup to first insight
in under 10 minutes.

No SDK wrestling. No data engineering tickets. Just three steps.

1

Connect your data

Drop in a warehouse connection or install our SDK in one line. Lumen auto-detects your schema and suggests a starting model.

2

Ask your first question

Type a question in plain English. Lumen generates the SQL, runs it, and returns a chart and a one-paragraph summary.

3

Share with your team

Pin the chart to a dashboard, share a link in Slack, or schedule a weekly digest. Decisions move faster when everyone sees the same numbers.
