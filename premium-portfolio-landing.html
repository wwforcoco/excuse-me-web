About — 01

A studio of one, with the reach of many.

I operate as an independent practice — pairing the focus of a solo designer with a trusted network of engineers, motion artists, and writers.

For eight years I've helped studios, founders, and cultural institutions translate ambitious ideas into coherent visual systems — from the first sketch to the last pixel shipped.

My work lives at the seam between brand and product, where typography, motion, and code meet. I care less about trends and more about clarity, craft, and the quiet details that make a thing feel inevitable.

Previously at Pentagram and Linear. Now building Maya Chen Studio — selective by design.

Process — 04

How the work happens.

A four-phase rhythm refined over eight years. Each phase ends with a decision — never a surprise, always a checkpoint.

01

Discover

We start with conversations — not briefs. Stakeholder interviews, audits, competitive mapping, and a shared definition of what success looks like.

Duration1–2 weeks
OutputResearch brief
02

Define

Strategy sharpens into direction. We align on positioning, principles, scope, and the singular idea the work will orbit around.

Duration1 week
OutputDirection doc
03

Design

The longest phase. We explore broadly, then converge — sharing work weekly, prototyping in code where it matters, refining until it sings.

Duration3–6 weeks
OutputDesign system
04

Deliver

We ship — together. Production-ready files, documented systems, a launch plan, and a 30-day window to support the rollout.

Duration1–2 weeks
OutputLaunch + docs
