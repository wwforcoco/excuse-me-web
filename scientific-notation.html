Frequently Asked Questions

What is scientific notation?

Scientific notation expresses a number as a coefficient (between 1 and 10) multiplied by a power of 10. For example, 6.022 × 10²³ represents 602,200,000,000,000,000,000,000.

When should I use scientific notation?

Scientific notation is used for very large numbers (like distances in astronomy) or very small numbers (like atomic sizes) to make them easier to read and write. It is standard in scientific and engineering fields.

How do I enter scientific notation?

You can enter it as a decimal number like 0.000045 or in E notation like 4.5e-5. The calculator auto-detects the format and converts to both forms.
