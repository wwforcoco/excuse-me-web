Frequently Asked Questions

What is keyword density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a keyword or phrase appears in a text relative to the total number of words. It is calculated as (number of keyword occurrences / total words) x 100. Search engines use it as one of many signals to determine a page's relevance.

What is the ideal keyword density percentage?

Most SEO experts recommend a primary keyword density between 1% and 3%. Going above 3% risks being flagged as keyword stuffing. The focus should be on natural, readable content rather than hitting a specific percentage.

What is keyword stuffing?

Keyword stuffing is the practice of loading a webpage with keywords in an unnatural way to manipulate search rankings. Search engines penalize this practice. Examples include repeating keywords out of context, hiding keywords in the page, or adding irrelevant keywords.
