Frequently Asked Questions

What is GST?

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is a value-added tax levied on most goods and services. It is charged at the point of sale and collected by businesses on behalf of the government.

What is the difference between GST and VAT?

GST and VAT are essentially the same concept—a consumption tax. The difference is mainly in naming. India uses GST, the EU uses VAT, and other countries use sales tax. All function similarly.

Who charges GST?

Registered businesses collect GST from customers on taxable goods and services and remit it to the government. End consumers bear the final cost, while businesses can claim input tax credits.
