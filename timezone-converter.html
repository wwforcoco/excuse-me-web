Convert Time Between Time Zones

This time zone converter lets you instantly convert any time from one time zone to another. Whether you are scheduling a meeting with colleagues in another country, planning a call with an overseas client, or figuring out what time a live event starts in your region, this tool gives you the answer in one click.

How to Use

Select the source time zone from the "From" dropdown, enter the time you want to convert, then select the target time zone from the "To" dropdown. The converted time, date, and UTC offset are displayed immediately. You can also swap the two zones with one click to reverse the conversion.

Supported Time Zones

The tool includes over 40 major cities and regions spanning the Americas, Europe, Asia, Africa, and Oceania. All conversions use the IANA time zone database and automatically account for daylight saving time changes throughout the year.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the time zone converter work?

The converter uses your browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat API with IANA time zone identifiers. Select a source time zone, enter a time, then select a target zone. The conversion happens instantly in your browser with no server calls.

Does it account for daylight saving time?

Yes. The browser automatically handles daylight saving time (DST) transitions for each time zone, so the converted time reflects the correct offset for the given date.

What is the difference between UTC and GMT?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the standard time reference used worldwide. GMT (Greenwich Mean Time) is a time zone used in the UK during winter. In practice, UTC and GMT are the same for most purposes, but UTC is a time standard while GMT is a time zone.

Can I convert between any two time zones?

Yes. You can select any two time zones from the dropdown lists, which include over 40 major cities and regions worldwide. Enter any time and get the equivalent in the target zone.
