This currency converter supports 15 of the most traded world currencies. Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the exchange rates?

The rates in this converter are approximate hardcoded values and should be used for estimation purposes only. They do not reflect real-time market rates. For exact conversions, use a live-rate service or check with your bank.

Does this use live data?

No. This converter uses hardcoded approximate exchange rates, so it works instantly without needing any API or internet connection. This makes it fast and reliable for quick estimates, but not suitable for real financial transactions.

Which currencies are supported?

The converter supports 15 major currencies: USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), GBP (British Pound), JPY (Japanese Yen), CAD (Canadian Dollar), AUD (Australian Dollar), CHF (Swiss Franc), CNY (Chinese Yuan), INR (Indian Rupee), MXN (Mexican Peso), BRL (Brazilian Real), KRW (South Korean Won), SGD (Singapore Dollar), HKD (Hong Kong Dollar), and SEK (Swedish Krona).
