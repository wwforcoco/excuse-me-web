About the event

A conference for people who build the future.

FrontierConf is where the conversations that define the next year of technology actually happen — not on a stage removed from the audience, but in the hallways, workshops, and late-night demos between sessions.

Across three days and five tracks, you'll hear from researchers shipping production LLM systems, engineers running 50-million-user platforms, designers rethinking interface paradigms, and leaders building teams that ship — not just talk about shipping.

Every talk is curated. Every workshop is hands-on. Every evening ends with the kind of networking that turns into next year's partnerships, hires, and side projects.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

If something's not here, our team replies to every email within one business day.

FrontierConf 2026 runs September 15–17, 2026 at the Aurora Convention Center, 1 Harbor Boulevard, San Francisco, CA. Doors open at 8:00 AM PT each day, with the first keynotes starting at 9:00 AM.

Every pass includes access to all three days, all five tracks, the after-party, lunch and coffee, and the full session recording library. Higher tiers add workshop seats, mentor hours, networking access, and VIP experiences — see the tickets section above for the full breakdown.

Absolutely — every track runs in parallel and seating is first-come, first-served (VIP gets reserved front rows). Workshops require a seat reservation because capacity is limited; you can reserve when you check in or via the attendee app.

Full refunds are available up to 60 days before the event. Between 60 and 14 days out, tickets can be transferred to another attendee for a small fee. Within 14 days, transfers are still allowed but no refunds — we're happy to help with name changes via email.

Yes — every talk on every stage is recorded and published to the attendee library within 48 hours of the session. Recordings remain available to ticket holders for 12 months after the event. Workshops are not recorded, to keep them hands-on and candid.

Yes — we have partnered rates with three hotels within walking distance of the venue, plus discounted BART and airport shuttle codes. Full details and booking links are emailed to all ticket holders immediately after purchase.

Aurora Convention Center is fully ADA-compliant with step-free access to every stage and lab. We also provide ASL interpretation, live captioning on all main-stage talks, quiet rooms, and dietary accommodations at every meal — just note your needs at checkout or email us anytime.
