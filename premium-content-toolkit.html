Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the Premium Content Toolkit, how each tool works, and how to get the most out of it.

The Readability Analyzer uses two well-established formulas. Flesch Reading Ease is computed as 206.835 − 1.015 × (words/sentences) − 84.6 × (syllables/words), producing a score from 0 to 100 where higher means easier. Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level is computed as 0.39 × (words/sentences) + 11.8 × (syllables/words) − 15.59, giving an approximate U.S. grade level. Syllables are estimated by counting vowel groups in each word, which is accurate for the vast majority of English words. Complex words are those with three or more syllables that are not proper nouns.

The Content Rewriter applies rule-based transformations depending on the chosen mode. Simplify replaces complex words with simpler synonyms and splits long sentences at conjunctions. Formal expands contractions and swaps casual words for formal equivalents. Casual does the reverse, adding contractions and conversational connectors. Expand adds elaboration phrases and transitional cues. Shorten removes filler words and condenses verbose phrases. Bullet points splits the text into a bulleted list of its key sentences. All transformations happen locally — your text never leaves the browser.
