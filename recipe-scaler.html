    Recipe Scaler

    Easily scale any recipe to serve more or fewer people. Enter your ingredients with quantities and units, then set your desired number of servings. The tool calculates the adjusted amounts using a simple ratio.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does recipe scaling work?

    Recipe scaling works by calculating a ratio between your desired servings and the original servings, then multiplying every ingredient quantity by that ratio. For example, scaling from 4 to 8 servings uses a 2x multiplier.

    Does scaling work for all recipes?

    Scaling works well for most recipes, especially simple ones like soups, sauces, and casseroles. Baking recipes can be trickier because the chemistry of leavening agents, spices, and pan sizes may need adjustment. For baked goods, it's best to scale in moderate amounts.

    What about spices and seasonings when scaling?

    Spices and seasonings don't always scale linearly. When doubling a recipe, you may want to use only 1.5x the spices, then taste and adjust. Large-scale scaling can make spices overwhelming, so always taste as you go.
