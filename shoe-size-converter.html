Frequently Asked Questions

Are shoe size conversions exact?

No, shoe size conversions are approximate because sizing systems vary between brands and even within a brand's different models. This tool uses standard conversion charts, but you should always check the specific brand's size guide when possible.

Why do different brands have different sizes?

There is no universal standard for shoe sizing. Each brand uses its own lasts (the mold used to shape the shoe) and has its own interpretation of sizing. This is especially true between European, American, and Asian manufacturers.

What is the difference between men's and women's shoe sizes?

In the US system, women's sizes are typically 1.5 sizes larger than men's. So a US Men's 8 is approximately a US Women's 9.5. However, men's and women's shoes may also differ in width and overall shape.
