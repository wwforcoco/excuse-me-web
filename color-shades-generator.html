Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between shades, tints, and tones?

Shades are created by adding black to a color (making it darker). Tints add white (making it lighter). Tones add gray (reducing saturation while keeping the hue).

How many shades are generated?

The tool generates 11 steps for each type: 10% increments from the original color to pure white/black/gray. Click any swatch to copy its HEX value.
