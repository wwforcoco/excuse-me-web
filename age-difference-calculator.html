Age Difference Calculator

Calculate the exact age difference between two people. Enter their birth dates and get a precise breakdown in years, months, and days, along with fun facts about their age gap including total days, weeks, hours apart, and more.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the age difference calculated?

The calculator finds the absolute difference between two birth dates, broken down into years, months, and days. It accounts for varying month lengths to give an accurate result.

Does this account for leap years?

Yes, the calculation uses JavaScript's built-in Date object which properly handles leap years. February 29th birthdays and leap year differences are calculated correctly.

What if both people have the same birthday?

If both dates are the same, the calculator will show 0 years, 0 months, 0 days difference and note that both people share the exact same birthday. It will still show fun facts like the day of the week they were born.
