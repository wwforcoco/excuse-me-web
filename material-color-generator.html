Frequently Asked Questions

What are Material Design color levels?

Material Design uses standardized lightness levels: 10 and 50 (lightest tints), 100-800 (progressively darker shades), 900 and 950 (darkest shades). These ensure visual consistency across UI components.

Can I use these colors in my project?

Yes, these colors are generated algorithmically and are free to use in any project. Click any swatch to copy its HEX value.
