Our Philosophy

Beauty, distilled
from the garden.

Éclat began in a small atelier in the sixth arrondissement, where our founder — a third-generation herbalist and a cosmetic chemist — set out to prove that luxury and clean formulation were never opposites. We harvest rare botanicals at their peak, cold-press them within hours, and balance each active with clinical precision.

Every formula is small-batch, vegan, and never tested on animals. We believe a ritual should feel as considered as it looks — that the bottle on your shelf is the start of a quiet, daily act of care.

C
Camille Aveline
Founder & Master Formulator

The Ritual

Three steps to luminous skin.

A morning and evening ritual, designed to layer with intention. Slow. Considered. Whole.

Step I
Mist

Prepare & Tone

Morning · 30 seconds

Mist Rose Mist across clean skin to set the canvas, balance pH and ready the barrier for what follows.

Step II
Serum

Treat & Correct

Morning & night · 1 minute

Press three drops of Rose Lumière serum between palms and pat into skin, focusing on tone and texture.

Step III
Crème

Seal & Soften

Night · 2 minutes

Warm a pea of Velvet Crème between fingertips and massage upward to lock in the ritual overnight.
