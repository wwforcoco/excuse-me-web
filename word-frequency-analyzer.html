Frequently Asked Questions

What are stop words?

Stop words are common words like 'the', 'is', 'at', 'which' that are often filtered out in text analysis because they carry little meaning on their own. Enabling this option shows more meaningful content words.

How is word frequency calculated?

Each word is converted to lowercase, stripped of punctuation, and counted. The tool then sorts words by frequency and shows the count and percentage of each word relative to the total.
