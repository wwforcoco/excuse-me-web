Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between the mortgage and loan calculators?

The mortgage calculator factors in property taxes and insurance alongside principal and interest, giving a more realistic monthly payment for homeowners. The loan calculator covers general installment loans like a car or personal loan.

Which conversion factors does the toolkit use?

All factors are international standard SI-to-imperial conversions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm, 1 foot = 0.3048 m, 1 mile = 1.60934 km, 1 lb = 0.453592 kg, 1 gallon (US) = 3.78541 L, and so on. Temperature uses the standard C/F/K formulas.

Can I use the budget template for shared household expenses?

Yes. The budget template has fields for income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and savings. Each partner can keep a separate copy, or you can list shared expenses in one template.

Does the mortgage calculator include PMI or HOA?

The base calculator includes principal, interest, taxes, and insurance (PITI). For PMI (Private Mortgage Insurance) and HOA fees, add them as separate monthly costs in the budget template.
