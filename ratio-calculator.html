Frequently Asked Questions

What is a ratio?

A ratio is a mathematical comparison of two or more quantities, showing how many times one value contains another. It is written as A:B or A/B. For example, a ratio of 3:2 means the first quantity is 1.5 times the second.

How do you simplify a ratio?

To simplify a ratio, divide both numbers by their greatest common divisor (GCD). For example, 12:8 simplifies to 3:2 because the GCD of 12 and 8 is 4, and 12÷4=3, 8÷4=2.

What is a proportion?

A proportion is an equation stating that two ratios are equal: A:B = C:D. If you know three of the four values, you can solve for the unknown. For example, if 3:4 = 6:x, then x = 8.
