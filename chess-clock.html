Online Chess Clock

This chess clock provides a two-player countdown with Fischer-style increment. Each player starts with the same amount of time and gains bonus seconds after each move. Tap or click your side of the board to pass control to your opponent.

How to Use

  1. Set the starting minutes and increment per move (e.g., 10+5 means 10 minutes with 5 seconds added per move).
  2. Click Start. Player 1's clock begins counting down.
  3. Player 1 clicks their side to stop their clock and start Player 2's clock.
  4. When a player's time reaches zero, an alarm sounds and the other player wins on time.

Features

  • Configurable starting time (1–120 minutes)
  • Fischer increment (0–60 seconds per move)
  • Visual active-turn highlighting
  • Red low-time warning under 30 seconds
  • Audio alarm on time expiry

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the chess clock work for two players?

Each player starts with the same amount of time. Press your side button to start your opponent clock and stop yours. Optional increment adds seconds after each move. When a player time reaches zero, they lose on time.

What is Fischer increment?

Fischer increment (named after Bobby Fischer) adds a fixed number of seconds to a player clock after each move. For example, with a 10+5 setting, each player starts with 10 minutes and gains 5 seconds after every move. This prevents time trouble in long games.

Can I use this for board games other than chess?

Yes. This chess clock works for any two-player turn-based game: checkers, Go, Scrabble, Stratego, or any game where players alternate turns. Set the starting time and increment to match your game.
