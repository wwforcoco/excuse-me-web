Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a sale price from a discount percentage?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage, then subtract that amount from the original price. The shortcut is Sale Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount % ÷ 100).

How do I find the discount percentage between two prices?

Subtract the sale price from the original price, divide by the original price, then multiply by 100. That gives you the percentage that was taken off.

How do I work out the original price from a sale price and discount?

Divide the sale price by (1 minus the discount as a decimal). For example, an $80 item marked 20% off had an original price of $80 ÷ 0.8 = $100.

Should a discount be calculated before or after sales tax?

Discounts are almost always applied to the pre-tax price first, with sales tax calculated on the reduced amount afterward. Check the receipt if the final total looks off.

How do I compare two different discount deals?

Convert every offer to its effective price per item or its effective percentage off, then compare those numbers directly. Deals like "buy one get one half off" often convert to a smaller effective discount than the headline suggests.

What's the difference between a percentage discount and a fixed dollar discount?

A percentage discount removes a proportion of the price, so the dollar savings grow with a higher price. A fixed discount, like "$10 off," removes the same amount regardless of price, so it's worth proportionally more on cheaper items.
