★ Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Flux ships with the features power users demand and the calm simplicity everyone loves — all wrapped in a buttery-smooth interface.

Sync

Smart Cloud Sync

Pick up exactly where you left off — across phone, tablet and desktop. Changes land in under 200ms, conflict-free.

Focus

Focus Mode

Silence the noise. One tap dims distractions, schedules deep work and protects your flow with gentle nudges.

Team

Team Collaboration

Share lists, assign tasks and leave comments in real time. Built for families, small teams and growing studios.

Secure

End-to-end Encryption

Your data is encrypted on-device before it ever leaves. Zero-knowledge by design — even we can't read it.

Custom

Custom Themes

Make Flux yours. Craft gradients, pick fonts, design widgets and share your look with the Flux community.

Offline

Offline First

No signal? No problem. Flux works fully offline and syncs the moment you reconnect — nothing lost, ever.
