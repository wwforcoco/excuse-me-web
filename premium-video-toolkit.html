Pacing & Speaking Rate Tips

Choose the rate that matches your delivery style and audience.

1
120 WPM — slow, deliberate. Best for tutorials, technical explainers, and educational content where viewers need to absorb details.
2
150 WPM — natural conversational pace. The YouTube default. Works for most vlogs, reviews, and how-tos.
3
180 WPM — fast and energetic. Ideal for entertainment, gaming, and reaction content where momentum matters.
4
200+ WPM — very fast. Use sparingly. Viewers may struggle to follow dense information at this pace.
5
Cut 10% after writing. Most scripts benefit from a trim pass — tighter pacing keeps retention higher.
6
Watch the first 30s — your opening should be 60–90 words max. Don't burn retention on a slow intro.
