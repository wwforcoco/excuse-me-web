Frequently Asked Questions

How does Google truncate titles and descriptions?

Google typically truncates title tags at around 600 pixels wide (approximately 50-60 characters) and meta descriptions at around 920 pixels (approximately 155-160 characters). The exact character count varies because Google measures by pixel width, not character count. Wider characters like 'W' take more space than narrow ones like 'i'.

Why should I keep my title under 60 characters?

Keeping your title under 60 characters ensures it displays fully in Google search results without being cut off with an ellipsis. A fully visible title improves click-through rates because users can see your complete headline and understand the page topic at a glance.

What is SERP?

SERP stands for Search Engine Results Page. It's the page displayed by Google (or other search engines) in response to a query. A SERP includes organic results, paid ads, featured snippets, knowledge panels, and other elements. Optimizing for SERP appearance helps improve visibility and click-through rates.
