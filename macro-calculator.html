Macro Nutrient Calculator

This calculator breaks down your daily calorie intake into three macronutrients — protein, carbohydrates, and fat — based on a selected preset ratio. You can adjust your base calories and select a goal (maintain, lose, or gain) to see the adjusted macro targets.

Macro Split Presets

  • Balanced (30/40/30): Good for general health and maintenance. Moderate protein, higher carbs for energy, moderate fat.
  • Low Carb (40/30/30): Higher protein, reduced carbs. Popular for fat loss while preserving muscle.
  • High Protein (40/30/30): Same as low carb — prioritizes protein for muscle building and satiety.
  • Keto (5/25/70): Very low carb, high fat. Designed for ketosis.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are macronutrients?

Macronutrients (macros) are the three main types of nutrients that provide energy: protein (4 cal/g), carbohydrates (4 cal/g), and fat (9 cal/g). Your body needs all three in varying amounts for optimal health, muscle repair, and energy production.

How much protein do I need per day?

The general recommendation is 0.8g per kg of body weight for sedentary adults. For active individuals, 1.2–2.0g per kg is often recommended. Athletes and those in a calorie deficit may benefit from even higher intake (1.6–2.2g per kg) to preserve muscle mass.

What is the keto diet?

The ketogenic (keto) diet is a very low-carb, high-fat diet. Typically it involves consuming 5% of calories from carbs, 25% from protein, and 70% from fat. This puts the body into a state of ketosis, where it burns fat for fuel instead of carbohydrates.

Can I customize the macro percentages?

This calculator provides preset splits for convenience. For a custom split, use the results as a starting point and manually adjust. A registered dietitian can help you determine the optimal macro ratio for your specific needs and health conditions.
