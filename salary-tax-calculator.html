Salary Tax Calculator

This calculator estimates your take-home pay after applying a flat tax rate to your annual salary. It provides a clear breakdown of your gross salary, tax amount, and net pay across different pay periods: annually, monthly, biweekly, and weekly. This is useful for quick budgeting and understanding how much of your salary goes to taxes.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this salary tax calculator?

This calculator provides a rough estimate based on a single flat tax rate. Real-world taxes involve progressive tax brackets, deductions, credits, Social Security, Medicare, state taxes, and more. For precise figures, consult a tax professional or use official tax software.

Does this include deductions like Social Security and Medicare?

No. This is a simple flat-rate estimator. It does not account for FICA taxes (Social Security 6.2%, Medicare 1.45%), state income taxes, health insurance premiums, 401(k) contributions, or other payroll deductions. You can adjust the tax rate to approximate your total effective rate.

What is the difference between federal and state income tax?

Federal income tax is collected by the IRS and applies to all US earners using progressive brackets. State income tax varies by state — some have no income tax (like Texas and Florida), while others have their own brackets and rates. This calculator uses a single combined rate for simplicity.
