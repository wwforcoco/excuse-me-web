Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a blank line?

A blank line is any line that contains zero characters (truly empty) or only whitespace characters such as spaces, tabs, and non-breaking spaces.

What are consecutive blank lines?

Consecutive blank lines are multiple blank lines appearing one after another. Removing consecutive blanks keeps a single blank line as a paragraph separator while removing extras.

Does removing blank lines affect my formatting?

It depends. Removing all blank lines removes paragraph separation. Removing consecutive blanks preserves paragraph breaks while cleaning up extra spacing. Use the option that fits your needs.
