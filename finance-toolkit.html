Frequently Asked Questions

How does the loan calculator compute monthly payments?

It uses the standard amortization formula: M = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments.

Why does the compound interest calculator show more than my bank's quote?

Banks often quote Annual Percentage Yield (APY) which already includes compounding. If you enter the nominal annual rate and pick a compounding frequency, the tool will arrive at the same APY. Make sure you're comparing like with like.

Where does the currency converter get its rates?

Currency rates are loaded from a public exchange-rate API at the moment you open the converter. The rates are indicative and may differ slightly from what your bank or card network charges, which usually adds a spread.

Is my financial data sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation happens in your browser tab. The numbers you type never leave your device, which makes the toolkit safe to use for personal budgeting.
