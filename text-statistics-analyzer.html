Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Flesch-Kincaid readability score?

The Flesch-Kincaid score measures text readability on a scale of 0-100. Higher scores indicate easier readability. Scores above 60 are considered easily understood by 13-15 year old students. The formula uses average sentence length and average syllables per word.

What does the Gunning Fog index measure?

The Gunning Fog index estimates the years of formal education needed to understand the text on the first reading. It accounts for sentence length and complex words (3+ syllables). A score of 6-8 is ideal for general audiences.

How is average word length calculated?

Average word length is the total number of letters in all words divided by the total number of words, excluding spaces and punctuation from the letter count.

What is lexical density?

Lexical density is the percentage of unique words (content words) in your text. Higher density means more varied vocabulary. Typical English text has 40-50% lexical density.
