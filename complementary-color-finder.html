Frequently Asked Questions

What is a complementary color?

A complementary color is directly opposite on the color wheel (180° apart). It creates the strongest contrast and visual impact when paired together.

What is an analogous color scheme?

Analogous colors are adjacent on the color wheel (30° apart). They create harmonious, serene designs that are pleasing to the eye.
