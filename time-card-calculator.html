Calculate Your Work Hours

This time card calculator makes it easy to total up your work hours for the week. Enter your clock-in and clock-out times for each day, add your break duration, and the calculator handles the rest — including overnight shifts that cross midnight.

How to Use

For each workday, enter the time you started (clock in) and the time you finished (clock out). If you took a break, enter the break duration. The calculator shows the net hours worked for each day and a running weekly total. You can add or remove rows to match your schedule.

Features

  • Supports overnight shifts (clock out after midnight)
  • Break time subtraction per day
  • Automatic daily and weekly totals
  • Overtime hours highlighted above 40 per week
  • Clear all or remove individual rows

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my work hours for the week?

Enter your clock-in and clock-out times for each day. The calculator subtracts break time and shows the total hours worked each day and the cumulative weekly total. Use the Add Row button to add more days.

Does the calculator handle overnight shifts?

Yes. If your clock-out time is earlier than your clock-in time (for example, 10 PM to 6 AM), the calculator assumes the shift crosses midnight and calculates the correct duration.

Can I subtract lunch or break time?

Yes. There is a break duration field for each day. Enter your break duration in hours and minutes, and it will be subtracted from the total shift time.

How is overtime calculated?

The calculator shows your total hours for the week. Any hours above 40 per week are highlighted separately. Overtime rules vary by location, so check your local labor laws for exact overtime rates.
