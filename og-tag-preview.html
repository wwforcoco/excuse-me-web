OG Tag Preview & Generator

Open Graph (OG) tags control how your web pages appear when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter. This tool lets you preview the social media card and generate the correct HTML meta tags to add to your page's <head> section.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are Open Graph (OG) tags?

Open Graph tags are meta tags introduced by Facebook that control how your content appears when shared on social media platforms. They define the title, description, image, and other properties that social networks use to create rich link previews.

Which platforms use Open Graph tags?

OG tags are primarily used by Facebook, LinkedIn, Twitter (as fallback for Twitter Cards), Pinterest, and other social platforms. Most major social networks that generate link previews from URLs will read and use Open Graph meta tags.

Do I really need OG tags?

While not strictly required, OG tags are highly recommended for any website. Without them, social platforms will try to guess the best title, description, and image from your page content, often with poor results. Proper OG tags ensure your shared links look professional and compelling.
