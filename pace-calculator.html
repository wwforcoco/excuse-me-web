Frequently Asked Questions

What is running pace?

Running pace is the time it takes to cover one unit of distance, typically expressed as minutes per kilometer (min/km) or minutes per mile (min/mi). For example, a 5:00 min/km pace means you run 1 km in 5 minutes.

How do I measure my running pace?

Use a GPS watch, smartphone running app (like Strava or Nike Run Club), or a treadmill. Most devices track distance and time automatically and calculate your pace in real time.

What is a good pace for beginners?

For beginners, a comfortable running pace is typically 6:00–7:30 min/km (9:40–12:00 min/mi). Start with a run-walk approach and gradually increase your running portions. The best pace is one where you can hold a conversation.
