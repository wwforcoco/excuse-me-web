Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 25% of 200 = (25 × 200) / 100 = 50. You can also convert the percentage to a decimal (25% = 0.25) and multiply: 0.25 × 200 = 50.

What percent is X of Y?

Divide X by Y and multiply by 100. For example, to find what percent 30 is of 150: (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%. This tells you that 30 is 20% of 150.

X is what percent of Y?

This is the same calculation as "what percent is X of Y." Divide X by Y and multiply by 100. The result tells you the percentage that the first number represents relative to the second. If X = 75 and Y = 200, then 75 is 37.5% of 200.
