Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between reversing text and word order?

Reversing text flips every character. Reversing word order keeps words intact but changes their sequence.

Why reverse text?

Common uses include testing palindromes, decoding reversed strings, or creating mirror text effects.

Does it work with non-English text?

Yes, the reversal works on individual characters regardless of language or script.
