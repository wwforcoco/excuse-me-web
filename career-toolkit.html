Frequently Asked Questions

What's the difference between a resume and a CV?

A resume is a 1-2 page summary tailored to a specific job, focused on skills and achievements. A CV (Curriculum Vitae) is a longer, comprehensive document covering academic and professional history, used mainly for academic, research, or medical roles.

How long should a cover letter be?

A cover letter should be 250-400 words — about half a page to one full page. Three short paragraphs work well: an opening that names the role, a middle that connects your skills to the job, and a closing with a call to action.

How do I write a two-week resignation letter?

Keep it short and professional: state your last working day (typically two weeks from the date you send the letter), thank the company for the opportunity, and offer to help with the handover. Avoid venting or listing grievances.

What format do these templates download in?

Each template is a fillable web page. Type directly into the fields, then use your browser's Print option and choose 'Save as PDF' to export a ready-to-send document. No software install required.
