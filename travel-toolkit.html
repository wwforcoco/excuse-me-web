Frequently Asked Questions

Where do the currency rates come from?

The currency converter fetches live indicative rates from a public exchange-rate API each time you open the tool. Your bank or card network may charge a different rate, usually with a 1-3% spread.

How does the time zone converter handle daylight saving?

It uses your browser's built-in Intl.DateTimeFormat with the IANA time zone database, which automatically accounts for daylight saving time rules in every region.

What's a good tipping etiquette reference?

Tipping customs vary by country. In the US, 15-20% is standard for restaurants. In Japan, tipping is uncommon. In Europe, 5-10% is typical. The calculator lets you set any percentage and split between travelers.

Does the packing checklist save my list?

Yes. Items you add are saved in your browser's localStorage. If you use the same device and browser, your list persists between visits. Clear your browser data and the list will reset.
