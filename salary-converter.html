💰 Salary Converter

Convert your salary between hourly, daily, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and annual pay periods.

Salary Converter Tool

The Salary Converter is a comprehensive tool that translates your pay between all common pay periods. Whether you are comparing job offers with different pay structures, negotiating a raise, or budgeting for the month, understanding your equivalent pay across periods is crucial.

Enter your salary amount and select the pay period to instantly see your equivalent earnings as hourly, daily, weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly, monthly, and annual figures. This makes it easy to compare different compensation packages on an equal footing.

The calculator uses standard assumptions of 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year. It is a handy reference for job seekers, employees, freelancers, and HR professionals who need to quickly convert between pay formats.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many work hours are assumed per year?

This calculator assumes 40 hours per week and 52 weeks per year (2,080 total work hours). This does not account for holidays, vacation, or unpaid time off.

What is the difference between bi-weekly and semi-monthly?

Bi-weekly pay occurs every two weeks (26 paychecks per year). Semi-monthly pay occurs twice per month on specific dates (24 paychecks per year). Semi-monthly paychecks are slightly larger.

Does this include taxes?

No, this calculator shows gross (pre-tax) pay amounts. Your actual take-home pay will be lower after federal, state, and local taxes, plus deductions for insurance and retirement.
