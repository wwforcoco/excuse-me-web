🖨️ Print Tips

Clean PDF Export Checklist

  1. Fill in every field before printing.
  2. Press Ctrl+P (Cmd+P on Mac) to open the print dialog.
  3. Set Destination to "Save as PDF".
  4. Set Paper Size to A4 or Letter as needed.
  5. Set Margins to "None" or "Default" — avoid "Minimum".
  6. Disable "Headers and footers" to remove browser URL and date.
  7. Enable "Background graphics" if the template has color accents.
  8. Click Save and choose where to save the PDF.

Troubleshooting

  • Fields cut off? Switch paper orientation to Landscape.
  • Colors missing? Enable "Background graphics" in print options.
  • Browser URL on page? Disable "Headers and footers".
  • Field text overflowing? Use shorter text or reduce font size in the editor.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I export a template to PDF?

Open any template, fill in the fields, then press Ctrl+P (or Cmd+P on Mac) and choose 'Save as PDF' as the destination. The templates are pre-formatted to print cleanly on A4 or Letter paper.

Can I edit a PDF after saving it?

The PDFs you export are standard PDF files. To edit them, use a PDF editor like Adobe Acrobat, Preview (Mac), or any free PDF tool. To re-export from this site, just open the template again and update the fields.

Are these templates legally binding?

The contract and letter templates are general formats. For jurisdiction-specific legal documents, have a lawyer review the final text. The templates give you a clean starting point but are not legal advice.

Can I customize the templates?

Yes. Each template is a standard HTML file. Save the page (Ctrl+S), open it in any text editor, and adjust the layout, colors, or fields to match your brand.
