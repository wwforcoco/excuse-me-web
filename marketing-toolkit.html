Frequently Asked Questions

How does the AI prompt generator work?

It combines curated prompt templates with your subject and tone inputs to produce structured prompts suitable for image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E), chat assistants (ChatGPT, Claude), or writing tools. The output is plain text you can copy and paste.

What's the difference between a static and dynamic QR code?

A static QR code encodes your text directly into the pattern, so it works forever with no service required. A dynamic QR code points to a redirect link managed by a provider, which lets you change the destination later but stops working if that provider shuts down.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram allows up to 30, but engagement usually peaks at 9-11 highly relevant tags. Twitter and LinkedIn perform best with 1-2 hashtags. The generator suggests relevant tags, but you should filter them for relevance to your post.

Can I use the meme generator commercially?

The meme templates built into the generator use common public-domain formats. If you upload your own image, you're responsible for having the rights to it. The output PNG is yours to use however you like.
