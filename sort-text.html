Frequently Asked Questions

Can I sort numbers?

Lines are sorted alphabetically. For numeric sorting, ensure all numbers have the same digit count with leading zeros.

Does it preserve blank lines?

Blank lines are included and sort to the top.

Can I sort in reverse?

Yes, use the Z-A or Long to Short buttons for descending order.
